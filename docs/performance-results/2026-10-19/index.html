Scope and limits

The hidden workload animates 100 elements and runs a timer on Discord's logged-out page. CPU is the sum of Electron process counters. Animation callbacks paused in every candidate trial. This does not measure battery life, logged-in scrolling, or call quality.

The patcher replay uses 11,282 captured module factories that match none of 18 remaining patterns. It reports the upper median of 20 alternating trials. It excludes successful replacements and whole-app startup. First-window timings include automation. Working-set sums can double-count shared pages. CI runners are not personal computers.

Both native installer suites passed, including local WebRTC echo while hidden. Physical-device voice/video and sharing audio still need testing. These comparisons do not establish a performance lead over Legcord or Vesktop.
